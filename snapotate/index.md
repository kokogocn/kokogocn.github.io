# Capture. Annotate. Done.

<p class="lead">Snapotate is a screenshot and annotation app that lives in your Mac's menu bar.
Press a shortcut, mark up what you captured, and copy or save it — all from the keyboard.</p>

- **Capture a region or the full screen** with a global shortcut or from the menu bar.
- **Annotate instantly** with text, rectangles, ellipses, arrows and a pen, in any color.
- **Copy or save in one keystroke** — <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> to paste anywhere, <kbd>⌘</kbd>
  <kbd>S</kbd> to save as PNG or JPEG.
- **Never lose a capture** — if Snapotate quits before you save, it offers your capture back.
- **Completely private** — no accounts, no tracking, and no internet connection at all.
  [Read the privacy policy](privacy/).

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Need help? Visit [Support](support/).
