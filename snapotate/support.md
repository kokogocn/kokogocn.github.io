# Snapotate Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). It helps to
include your macOS version, your Snapotate version (menu bar icon → About Snapotate), and what you
were doing when the problem happened.

## Getting started

1. Open Snapotate. Its **S** icon appears in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
2. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> and drag across the part of the screen you want.
3. The first time, macOS asks for **Screen Recording** permission. Turn it on for Snapotate, then
   quit and reopen Snapotate — macOS usually offers a **Quit & Reopen** button for this.

Your capture opens in the editor, ready to mark up. Copy it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> or save
it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>.

## Keyboard shortcuts

### Capturing

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Capture a region | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> |
| Capture the full screen | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>2</kbd> |
| Cancel a region selection | <kbd>Esc</kbd> |

You can change both shortcuts in Settings → General. A full-screen capture takes the display your
pointer is on.

### In the editor

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Select, Text, Rectangle, Ellipse, Arrow, Pen | <kbd>V</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd> |
| Draw a perfect square or circle, or a 45° arrow | Hold <kbd>⇧</kbd> while drawing |
| Move the selected annotation | Arrow keys (hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> for smaller steps) |
| Delete the selected annotation | <kbd>Delete</kbd> |
| Edit a text box again | Double-click it with the Select tool |
| Undo / Redo | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> / <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> |
| Copy the image | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> |
| Save / Save As | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> / <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> |
| Close the editor | <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> |

To add text, choose the Text tool, click where you want it and type. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> when
you're done.

## Common questions

### Nothing happens when I press the shortcut

Another app may already be using that shortcut — Snapotate tells you when it can't use one. Choose
a different shortcut in Settings → General. You can always capture from the menu bar icon too.

### Snapotate keeps asking for Screen Recording permission

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and make sure Snapotate
is switched on, then quit and reopen Snapotate. macOS only picks up the change after a restart of
the app.

### Where are my screenshots saved?

In **Pictures → Snapotate**, unless you've chosen another folder. Click the menu bar icon and choose
**Open Save Folder** to go straight there. You can change the folder, the file name format, and PNG
or JPEG in Settings → Saving.

### Can I copy a screenshot without saving it?

Yes. Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> in the editor and paste it wherever you like. You can close the
editor afterwards without being asked to save.

### Snapotate quit before I saved my capture

Snapotate keeps a backup of every capture until you save or discard it. If it quits first, it
offers to reopen the capture the next time it starts.

### Can I change annotations after saving?

No — annotations become part of the image when you save it. Until then you can move, resize, restyle
or delete anything, so keep the editor open until you're happy.

### Can I capture across two displays?

A region capture covers the display where you start dragging.

### How do I uninstall Snapotate?

Click the menu bar icon, choose **Quit Snapotate**, and move Snapotate to the Trash. To remove its
settings as well, follow [Deleting your data](../privacy/#deleting-your-data) in the privacy policy.

## Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs.
