# Snapotate Privacy Policy

**Effective October 9, 2026**

Snapotate is a screenshot and annotation app for macOS, made by Ke Bai. This policy explains
what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Snapotate does not collect, send or share any of your data. Your screenshots and settings stay
on your Mac. Snapotate only uses a screenshot to show it in its editor, keep a backup until
you're done with it, and save or copy it when you ask.

## Nothing leaves your Mac

- **No internet connection.** Snapotate runs in Apple's App Sandbox without network access, so
  macOS itself prevents it from connecting to the internet.
- **No analytics, tracking or advertising.** Snapotate doesn't record how you use it.
- **No accounts.** There's nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
- **No third-party code.** Snapotate is built only on Apple's own frameworks, so no outside
  service is involved.

## What Snapotate keeps on your Mac

| What | Where | When it's removed |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots you save | The folder you choose — `Pictures/Snapotate` by default | Only when you delete them |
| A backup of each new capture, so nothing is lost if the app quits unexpectedly | Snapotate's private app folder | As soon as you save, copy and close, or discard the capture. If Snapotate quits first, it offers the capture back the next time it starts. |
| Your settings, such as shortcuts, save folder, file name format, and annotation colors and sizes | Snapotate's private app folder | When you delete that folder (see below) |

When you copy a screenshot, it goes onto the macOS clipboard, where other apps can paste it — as
with anything you copy.

## Permissions

- **Screen Recording** — needed to take screenshots. Snapotate only captures your screen when you
  start a capture with a keyboard shortcut or from its menu. You can turn this off at any time in
  System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
- **Files and folders** — Snapotate can only save to your Pictures folder and to folders you pick
  yourself.
- **Launch at login** — off unless you turn it on in Snapotate's settings.

## Apple

Snapotate is distributed through the Mac App Store. Downloads and purchases are handled by Apple
under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and I never receive your
payment details.

If you've chosen to share data with app developers (System Settings → Privacy & Security →
Analytics & Improvements → Share with app developers), Apple may share crash reports and usage
statistics for Snapotate with me, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. I use these only to fix
problems and improve Snapotate.

## Deleting your data

Because everything is on your Mac, you're in full control of it:

1. Delete any screenshots you've saved.
2. Quit Snapotate and move it to the Trash.
3. To remove its settings and any backed-up captures, delete the folder
   `~/Library/Containers/com.bai.snapotate` (in Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder…).

## Children

Snapotate doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
